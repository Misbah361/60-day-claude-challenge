Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the AI/ML Intern position at TechNova
Analytics. As a second-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence &

QA.

Beyond coursework, I am currently completing a self-driven 60-Day AI
Challenge applying prompt engineering, context engineering, and
chain-of-thought reasoning to build functional, data-driven
applications. This includes dashboards with real data integration,
filtering logic, and structured pipelines, along with a
nutrition-tracking application where I used Pandas for data cleaning and
fuzzy matching across inconsistent datasets. I document every project on
GitHub with structured technical write-ups, reflecting both my technical
growth and my communication discipline.

I am proficient in Python, SQL, NumPy, and Pandas, with working
knowledge of Flask and Git/GitHub, and I am actively building toward
machine learning fundamentals. What I bring to your team is not just
technical foundation, but a demonstrated pattern of independently
scoping, building, and shipping complete projects --- a habit I believe
translates directly into strong intern performance and rapid onboarding.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my simulation engineering
background and applied AI learning can contribute to your team's data
science initiatives. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely, Misbah Fayaz

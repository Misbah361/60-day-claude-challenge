Recent Governance Note
  • MD & CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan announced on 29 Aug 2026 that he will not seek reappointment and will retire on 26 October 2026, with a successor search underway.
  • Completed a US$1.75 billion senior unsecured bond issue via GIFT City (Aug 2026); rated BBB/Baa3 by S&P and Moody's.
Strengths
  • Dominant, entrenched market position — 2nd-largest bank in India, leader across most asset categories.
  • Strong long-run compounding: 19% profit CAGR over 10 years.
  • Excellent recent cash conversion — CFO/Operating Profit near 94–98% in FY25–FY26.
  • Diversified, high-grade credit rating profile (BBB/Baa3 international) supporting cheap global funding access.
Watch-Points
  • ROE has structurally declined from 20% to 14% over a decade — now below ICICI Bank and SBI.
  • Steady FII outflow (52%→42% since 2023) alongside rising DII ownership — a sentiment signal worth monitoring.
  • CEO transition in October 2026 introduces near-term leadership uncertainty.
  • Low interest coverage ratio and large contingent liabilities flagged — largely structural for a bank this size, but worth understanding rather than ignoring.
Key Metric to Track

Quarterly ROE trend and CASA ratio (deposit mix) over the next 2–3 quarters — these will show whether the post-merger profitability compression is stabilizing or continuing.
